# Product Guidelines

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional & Direct:** All system communications, including labels, tooltips, and messages, must be clear, concise, and efficient. The focus is on facilitating task completion without unnecessary conversational filler, suitable for a financial and administrative tool.

## Visual Identity & Aesthetic
- **Clean & Minimalist:** The user interface should prioritize data clarity and reduce cognitive load. This is achieved through high contrast, generous use of white space, and a strong emphasis on readable typography.

## Error Handling & Feedback
- **Action-Oriented Messaging:** Error messages must be constructive. Instead of simply stating a failure, they should provide clear instructions on how the user can resolve the issue (e.g., "Please enter a valid email address" rather than "Invalid input").

## Data Presentation Standards
- **Standardized Formatting:** Financial data must be presented consistently. Dates should follow ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD), and currency amounts must always include two decimal places and the appropriate symbol.
- **Visual Hierarchies:** Use font weight and color strategically to draw the user's attention to critical information, such as the "Total Amount Due" or the "Due Date".
- **Interactive Summaries:** Complex forms or long lists should be preceded by clear, high-level summaries to provide immediate context and key takeaways.
