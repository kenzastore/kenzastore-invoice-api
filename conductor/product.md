# Initial Concept
A professional invoice management system featuring JWT authentication, CRUD operations for invoices, and automated PDF generation, originally developed for Kenzastore and now evolved into a self-hosted solution for freelancers and small business owners.

# Product Definition

## Target Audience
- **Freelancers and Small Business Owners:** Individuals and small teams looking for a professional, self-hosted invoicing solution that avoids the recurring costs of SaaS platforms while maintaining full control over their data.

## Core Goals
- **Professional Billing Automation:** Streamline the entire billing lifecycle through automated PDF generation and high-quality templates that project a professional image.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Empower users to host their own financial data, ensuring privacy and security.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduce the administrative burden of invoicing through smart workflows and integrations.

## Key Features
- **Recurring Invoices:** Support for automated, scheduled billing for subscription-based services or long-term contracts.
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Ability to generate invoices in various currencies to facilitate international business operations.
- **Payment Gateway Integration:** Seamless integration with popular providers like Stripe and PayPal, allowing clients to pay directly from the invoice.
- **JWT Authentication:** Secure, token-based access control for all API endpoints.

## User Experience & Workflow
- **Guided Invoice Wizard:** A step-by-step interface that simplifies the invoice creation process, reducing errors and saving time.
- **Quick-Create Templates:** Pre-defined templates for common services and products to enable rapid invoice generation.
- **Real-time PDF Preview:** Instant visual feedback that allows users to see exactly how the final invoice will look as they enter data.
